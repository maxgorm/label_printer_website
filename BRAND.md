# Brand Context

> Optional project context for blog sub-skills, loaded as untrusted data. Last updated: 2026-11-05.

## Audience

- **Primary**: Couples in long-distance relationships (mostly 18-30) who want to feel closer day to day, including people shopping for a gift for their partner
- **Secondary**: Families, for example grandparents who receive notes from grandkids on a shared single printer
- **Expertise**: Beginner (everyday consumers, not tech buyers)
- **Active problems**:
  - Texting feels fleeting; they want something they can actually hold on to
  - Long distance makes it hard to feel present in each other's day
  - Finding a meaningful, non-generic gift for anniversaries, Valentine's Day or birthdays
  - Staying in touch with grandparents or relatives who don't text much
- **Common misconceptions**:
  - "It needs ink." It uses thermal paper, with no ink ever.
  - "Both people need a printer." Only the receiver needs one; anyone can send from the app.
  - "There's a subscription." Sending and receiving notes is free.
  - "It's just another photo printer like an Instax." It receives messages from another person, remotely and instantly.

- **Mission**: Sentimo helps people who care about each other stay close by turning everyday messages into real, printed notes they can keep.
- **Distinctive POV**: A message you can hold means more than one you scroll past. Small, frequent physical moments build closeness better than big, occasional gestures.
- **What we are NOT**: Not a photo printer or camera, not a productivity, label or receipt gadget, and not a social network. Sentimo is private, one-to-one connection.
- **Competitors**:
  - Lovebox: messages appear on a small screen inside the box and are replaced by the next one; Sentimo prints every message as a sticker note to keep and stick anywhere, and the 2-pack gives both people a printer.
  - Bond Touch: the bracelets send a tap or vibration; Sentimo sends the actual words, drawings and photos.
  - Digital photo frames (Aura and similar): they cycle photos on a screen; Sentimo turns notes, doodles and photos into paper keepsakes, and anyone can send to it from a free app.

## Editorial Rules

### Always do
- Lead with real moments (a note on the mirror, a doodle inside a book), not product specs
- Use real photos of Sentimo prints; use real customer stories only with their permission
- Give genuinely useful advice that works even without Sentimo; mention the product where it naturally fits
- Keep product facts current: prices, shipping countries and paper yield must match the site
- Cite real sources for any statistic about relationships or long distance

### Never do
- Make up statistics, reviews or customer quotes
- Talk down competitors by name in a mean or inaccurate way (fair comparisons are fine)
- Use guilt or fear (e.g. "your relationship is failing without...")
- Write clickbait titles or pad articles with filler
- Make health or therapy claims about relationships

### Taboo phrases
- game-changer
- revolutionary
- cutting-edge
- bridge the distance
- love knows no distance
- in today's digital age
- unlock
- elevate

### Required disclosures
- In any post that compares Sentimo with competitors, state clearly that Sentimo wrote it
- No affiliate links for now
