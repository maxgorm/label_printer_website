## Open issues

### High
1. **Mobile LCP 6.8 s is caused by render-blocking Google Fonts.** Two font stylesheets block rendering (about 1.2 s of estimated savings). The Material Icons font is 174 KB and has no `display=swap`. Fix: load only the icons used (`&icon_names=` subset) with `&display=block`/`swap`, preload the DM Sans font, or self-host both. Check: mobile LCP under 2.5 s.
2. **Stripe.js is loaded on the homepage but never used** (259 KB, 191 KB of it unused). Checkout redirects to a Stripe-hosted URL, so the `<script src="https://js.stripe.com/v3/">` tag can go. That also removes the third-party cookie (`m.stripe.com`) that costs Best Practices points. Check: Best Practices 90+ and no Stripe requests on the homepage.
3. **The 73 KB logo PNG is the mobile LCP element**, yet it displays only 32-40 px tall. A 2x WebP (about 350x80) would be about 5 KB.

### Medium
4. **Gallery thumbnails** download the full 1254 px images (about 535 KB of waste on desktop). Fix: serve about 240 px thumbnail versions and keep the full images for the main carousel.
5. **The product video downloads about 3.3 MB on desktop** before anyone presses play. Fix: `preload="none"` (a poster is already set).
6. **Accessibility (89):**
   - `aria-checked` on `<label>` elements in the product and color pickers is not allowed. Remove it, since the inner radios already carry their state.
   - The pink brand color fails contrast. White on #F53F7B is 3.57:1 and pink on cream is 3.42:1, against 4.5:1 needed for normal text. Options: a darker CTA pink, or bold/larger text.
   - A heading order skip: an `<h4>` follows an `<h2>`.
7. **Thin meta descriptions:** Privacy has 22 chars, Terms 24 chars, FAQ 41 chars. The FAQ title is the generic "FAQ - Sentimo".
8. **Homepage title** is slogan-only. You asked to leave it for now.
9. **AI crawlers** (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and others) are blocked by Cloudflare's managed robots.txt. This is a policy choice; it limits AI-answer visibility.
10. **Content depth:** there is a single landing page, with no gift-guide or use-case pages.

### Low / Info
- Lighthouse reports `Content-Signal:` in robots.txt as an "unknown directive". It's Cloudflare's managed line and harmless to Google.
- No llms.txt (optional).
- A stale cached 404 sits on the bare `/assets/css/tailwind-main.css` URL. Pages use the `?v=` URL, so there's no visitor impact.
- Manual, needs Google sign-in: run the Rich Results Test and submit the sitemap in Search Console.
