## Pronoun stance
Mixed: second person ("you") for the reader, first-person plural ("we") for Sentimo.

## Lexical rules
- **Contractions**: full
- **Sentence ceiling**: 25 words max
- **Paragraph ceiling**: 80 words max
- **Summary label**: The short version

## Headline patterns
- **Favor**: clear statements ("What to send someone you miss"); numbered lists for gift guides ("15 long-distance anniversary gifts that aren't jewelry"); promises the article keeps
- **Avoid**: clickbait ("You won't believe..."); ALL CAPS; questions that just repeat the search query
