Order Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Sentimo orders are now shipping. We ship to addresses anywhere in the United States and to the international destinations listed below.

Payment

Payment is collected at the time you place your order.

Shipping

United States: shipping typically takes 2-5 days. Shipping is $4.99, or free on orders of $25 or more before tax.

International: shipping typically takes 5-14 days and is a flat fee per order:

  • Canada: $14.99
  • United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Mexico: $19.99
  • Italy, Spain, Sweden, Denmark, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, and Singapore: $24.99

We do not ship to countries not listed here. For international orders, import duties, taxes, and customs fees charged by the destination country are the buyer’s responsibility and are not included in the order total. A phone number is required at checkout for international orders so the carrier can complete customs forms.

Delivery times are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates.

Delays

Manufacturing, freight, customs, and fulfillment timelines can change. If there is a material delay to your order, we will notify you by email and provide an updated timeline. If required, customers will have the option to continue waiting or cancel for a refund.

Refunds

Orders are fully refundable at any time before shipment. If Sentimo is unable to fulfill your order, you will receive a full refund to your original payment method.

Contact

If you have questions about your order, contact us at: support@sentimonotes.com
