Frequently asked questions

Where do you ship?

We ship anywhere in the US, plus Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Denmark, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, and Singapore. US shipping is free on orders of $25 or more before tax. International orders pay a flat shipping fee from $14.99 to $24.99 depending on the country, and duties and taxes are the buyer’s responsibility.

When will orders ship?

Orders are shipping now. US delivery takes 2-5 days, and international delivery takes 5-14 days. We will email you tracking details once your order ships.

What if there is a delay?

If anything delays your order, we will email you with an update. If we cannot fulfill your order, you will receive a full refund.

What comes in the box?

Choose a single printer or a 2-pack. Each option includes the printer(s), starter paper, charging cables, and quick setup materials so you can begin sending notes right away.

Does it work with both iOS and Android?

Yes! The Sentimo app is available on both the App Store and Google Play Store. You can pair devices across different operating systems seamlessly.

Do I need ink?

Never! Sentimo uses thermal printing technology, meaning no ink, ever. You just need to refill the paper rolls when they run out.

How long does the paper last?

One roll of sticker thermal paper can print about 50-60 photos or 80-100 notes. The print itself lasts for years without fading if kept away from direct heat sources.

Can I print photos?

Absolutely. The app converts any photo from your gallery into a high-contrast black and white dithered image optimized for thermal printing.

Do I pay now or later?

Payment is completed at checkout.

Can I cancel before it ships?

Yes. Orders are fully refundable any time before shipment.

Is there a monthly subscription?

The core features of sending and receiving notes are completely free forever. We may offer optional premium features in the future, but the essentials will always be free.
